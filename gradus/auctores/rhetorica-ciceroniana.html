Gradus VII · Auctores

Rhetorica Ciceroniana: Figurae

Anaphora, tricolon, chiasmus, climax — nomina Latina pro artibus persuadendi.

PropositaExplicatioRegulaeExemplaErrores CommunesExercitiumSummariumTe Ipsum Proba

Prooemium

Cicerō nōn sōlum ōrāvit, sed etiam scrīpsit tractātūs dē rhētoricā ipsā (Dē Ōrātōre, Brūtus). Hodiē nōmina technica figūrārum quās iam vīdistī (sine nōmināre eās explicitē) discis, ut textūs futūrōs analysāre possīs professiōnāliter.

Post hanc lectionem poteris…

  • Nominare et definire sex figuras rhetoricas frequentes in prosa classica
  • Identificare has figuras in textibus iam lectis (Cicero, Sallustius, Vergilius)
  • Comprehendere propositum persuasivum/stilisticum cuiusque figurae
  • Applicare vocabularium technicum rhetoricae in analysi textus novi

Explicatio

Explicatio

Figūra rhētorica est structūra verbōrum dēlīberāta, adhibita nōn prō necessitāte grammaticā, sed prō effectū persuāsīvō vel aesthēticō.

Regulae Grammaticae

Regulae

a) Anaphora

Repetītiō verbī initiālis in clausulīs sequentibus: nihil...nihil...nihil...nihil...nihil (Cicerō, In Catilīnam I, iam vīsum). Effectus: accumulātiō ēmphatica.

b) Tricolon

Trēs elementa parallēla in sēriē: quid...ēgeris, ubi fuerīs, quōs convocāverīs (Cicerō). Effectus: rhythmus et complētūdō.

c) Chiasmus

Ōrdō verbōrum in patrōne A-B-B-A (crucifōrmis): animī imperiō, corporis servitiō (Sallustius — imperium/servitium invertunt ōrdinem respectū animī/corporis). Effectus: ēlegantia, balanceāta antithesis.

d) Asyndeton

Absentia coniunctiōnis expectātae inter elementa parallēla: lingua, īnstitūtīs, lēgibus (Caesar). Effectus: vēlōcitās, accumulātiō.

e) Hyperbaton

Sēparātiō dēlīberāta verbōrum grammaticē coniūnctōrum: saevae memorem Iūnōnis ob īram (Vergilius). Effectus: suspēnsiō, ēmphasis.

f) Climax (Gradatio)

Sēriēs verbōrum crēscentium in intēnsitāte: exemplum classicum (Cicerō, In Verrem II.5.170, verificātum verbātim): Facinus est vincīre cīvem Rōmānum, scelus verberāre, prope parricīdium necāre. ('It is a crime to bind a Roman citizen, a wickedness to flog him, almost a parricide to kill him.') Effectus: intēnsificātiō prōgressīva.

Exempla

Exempla in Usu

  • Nihil...nihil...nihil (Cicero) — anaphora.
  • Quid egeris, ubi fueris, quos convocaveris (Cicero) — tricolon.
  • Animi imperio, corporis servitio (Sallustius) — chiasmus.
  • Lingua, institutis, legibus (Caesar) — asyndeton.
  • Saevae memorem Iunonis ob iram (Vergilius) — hyperbaton.

Summarium

Recognitio

  • Anaphora: repetitio verbi initialis. Tricolon: tres elementa parallela.
  • Chiasmus: patronus A-B-B-A. Asyndeton: absentia coniunctionis expectatae.
  • Hyperbaton: separatio deliberata verborum coniunctorum. Climax: series crescens.
  • Omnes hae figurae iam visae sunt in textibus Caesaris, Ciceronis, Sallustii, et Vergilii lectis.
