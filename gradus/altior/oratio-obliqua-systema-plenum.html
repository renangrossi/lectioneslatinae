Gradus VI · Altior

Oratio Obliqua: Systema Plenum

AcI, imperativa obliqua, interrogationes obliquae — omnia coniuncta, cum coniunctivo obliquo.

PropositaExplicatioRegulaeExemplaErrores CommunesExercitiumSummariumTe Ipsum Proba

Prooemium

'Caesar dixit se, si Galli arma deponerent, pacem facturum esse,' legit Iulia ex libro. 'Cur deponerent est coniunctivus?' rogat. 'Quia intra orationem obliquam iacet,' respondet magister — omnis clausula subordinata fit coniunctivus.

Post hanc lectionem poteris…

  • Coniungere AcI, imperativa obliqua, et interrogationes obliquas in unum passum narrativum
  • Cognoscere coniunctivum obliquum: clausulae subordinatae intra oratione obliqua fiunt semper coniunctivo
  • Uti coniugatione periphrastica intra AcI (dicit id faciendum esse)
  • Vertere passum plenum orationis obliquae, secundum practicam auctorum classicorum (Caesar, Livius)

Explicatio

Explicatio

“Caesar dīxit sē, sī Gallī arma dēpōnerent, pācem factūrum esse,” legit Iūlia ex librō Dē Bellō Gallicō. “Cūr 'dēpōnerent' coniūnctīvus est?” rogat. “In ōrātiōne rēctā esset indicātīvus futūrum: 'sī dēpōnent'.” “Quia intrā ōrātiōne oblīquā iacet,” respondet magister.

Rēgula ultima et māximē important: intrā ōrātiōne oblīquā, OMNIS clausula subōrdināta (etiam illae quae in ōrātiōne rēctā adhibērent indicātīvum) fit CONIŪNCTĪVUS. Hoc vocātur coniūnctīvus oblīquus. Auctōrēs historicī (Caesar, Līvius) saepe reportant longōs sermōnēs integrōs sīc, combinantēs AcI, imperātīva oblīqua, et interrogātiōnēs oblīquae in ūnum passum flūminis.

Regulae Grammaticae

Regulae

a) Coniunctivus Obliquus: Regula Universalis

Ōrātiō rēcta: 'Cum Rōmam vēnī, templa vīdī.' ('When I came to Rome, I saw the temples' — indicātīvus 'vēnī', factum simplex.) Ōrātiō oblīqua: Dīxit sē, cum Rōmam vēnisset, templa vīdisse. ('He said that, when he had come to Rome, he had seen the temples.') — nōtā 'vēnisset' NUNC coniūnctīvus, quamvis in ōrātiōne rēctā fuisset indicātīvus simplex, quia intrā oblīquitāte tōtīus passūs iacet.

b) Combinatio: AcI + Coniunctivus Obliquus

Caesar dīxit sē, sī Helvētiī trānsīrent, bellum factūrum esse. ('Caesar said that, if the Helvetii crossed, he would make war.') — 'dīxit sē...factūrum esse' est AcI prīncipālis; 'sī...trānsīrent' est clausula condiciōnālis intrā, coniūnctīvō propter oblīquitātem.

c) Gerundivum intra AcI

Coniugātiō periphrastica passīva potest intrāre AcI normāliter: Dīxit id faciendum esse. ('He said that it must be done.')

d) Strategia Lectionis: Passus Longi

In textū authenticō, ōrātiō oblīqua longa (Caesar, Dē Bellō Gallicō, saepe capitula integra) exigit: (1) identificāre verbum dīcendī prīncipāle; (2) sequī omnēs īnfīnītīvōs AcI subsequentēs (saepe cōniūnctī per 'et'/'-que', sine verbō dīcendī repetītō); (3) recognōscere omnem coniūnctīvum ut oblīquum, nōn necessāriō fīnālem/cōnsecūtīvum/etc.

Exempla

Exempla in Usu

  • Caesar respondit se pacem velle, si Galli arma deponerent. — Caesar replied that he wanted peace, if the Gauls would lay down their arms.
  • Legati dixerunt milites, qui fortiter pugnavissent, praemium accepturos esse. — The envoys said that the soldiers, who had fought bravely, would receive a reward.
  • Nuntiavit urbem, quae diu obsessa esset, tandem cepisse. — He announced that the city, which had long been besieged, had finally been captured.
  • Dixit omnia, quaecumque fecisset, bono animo facta esse. — He said that everything he had done was done with good intent.
  • Negavit se scire quid facturi essent. — He denied knowing what they were going to do.

Errores Communes

Cave

VitaClausula relativa intra oratione obliqua ('qui fortiter pugnaverant') manet in indicativo, quia relativae normaliter sunt factuales.

RectiusIntra oratione obliqua, TOTA clausula subordinata (incluso relativa) fit coniunctivus obliquus: 'quī fortiter pugnāvissent'.

Coniunctivus obliquus applicatur universaliter, sine exceptione pro genere clausulae subordinatae.

VitaDiscipulus perdit filum narrativum in passibus longis, non recognoscens quod multi infinitivi pendent ab uno verbo dicendi initiali.

RectiusVerificare semper verbum dicendi principale (saepe solum semel explicitum), deinde sequi catenam infinitivorum/coniunctivorum obliquorum dependentium.

Prosa historica classica saepe non repetit verbum dicendi per omnem sententiam — structura debet inferri.

Summarium

Recognitio

  • Coniunctivus obliquus: omnis clausula subordinata intra oratione obliqua fit coniunctivus, sine exceptione.
  • AcI, imperativa obliqua, interrogationes obliquae, et clausulae subordinatae combinantur in passibus longis.
  • Gerundivum intrat AcI normaliter (dicit id faciendum esse).
  • Strategia lectionis: identifica verbum dicendi, sequere catenam infinitivorum/coniunctivorum.
