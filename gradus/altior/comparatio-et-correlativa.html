Gradus VI · Altior

Comparatio et Correlativa

Quanto...tanto — 'the more...the more', cum ablativo mensurae geminato.

PropositaExplicatioRegulaeExemplaErrores CommunesExercitiumSummariumTe Ipsum Proba

Prooemium

Cicerō, in ōrātiōnibus suīs, frequenter adhibet cōnstrūctiōnēs corrēlātīvās ēlegantēs prō comparātiōnibus proportiōnālibus — 'quantō magis..., tantō magis...' ('the more..., the more...').

Post hanc lectionem poteris…

  • Uti constructione quantō...tantō ('the more...the more')
  • Uti tam...quam ('as...as', aequalitas) et quam + superlativo ('as...as possible')
  • Distinguere correlativa comparationis ab aliis usibus quam
  • Recognoscere has constructiones in prosa argumentativa (Cicero)

Explicatio

Explicatio

Corrēlātīva combīnant duo comparātīva (vel duo verba intēnsīva) in ūnam sententiam proportiōnālem.

Regulae Grammaticae

Regulae

a) Quanto...Tanto: 'The More...The More'

Ambō ablātīvī mēnsūrae (Gradus VI, lēctiō 3), gubernantēs comparātīvōs: Quantō plūs habēmus, tantō plūra cupimus. ('The more we have, the more we desire.') Alternātīva: Quō...eō (idem sēnsus): Quō plūs habēs, eō plūra vīs.

b) Tam...Quam: Aequalitas

Exprimit aequalitātem (nōn comparātiōnem gradūs): Tam fortis est quam pater suus. ('He is as brave as his father.')

c) Quam + Superlativus: 'As...As Possible'

quam + superlātīvus (interdum cum 'possum'): quam celerrimē ('as quickly as possible'), quam plūrimī ('as many as possible').

d) Ut...Ita: Correlativum Concessivum

ut...ita potest exprimere concessiōnem corrēlātam: Ut fortis est, ita nōn semper sapiēns. ('While/although he is brave, he is not always wise.')

Exempla

Exempla in Usu

  • Quanto doctior est, tanto modestior. — The more learned he is, the more modest.
  • Tam pulchra est quam mater eius. — She is as beautiful as her mother.
  • Quam celerrime venite! — Come as quickly as possible!
  • Quo plus habemus, eo plus cupimus. — The more we have, the more we desire.
  • Ut fortis in bello, ita clemens in pace fuit. — As brave in war, so merciful in peace was he.

Errores Communes

Cave

Vita'Quantō...tantō' vertitur litteraliter 'by how much...by so much', sine idiomate Anglico apto.

RectiusTraductio idiomatica: 'the more...the more' — litteralis quoque intellegibilis, sed idioma est magis naturale.

Constructio Latina et Anglica sunt structuraliter parallelae (ambae correlativa), sed verba differunt idiomaticē.

Vita'Tam...quam' confunditur cum 'tam...ut' (clausula consecutiva, Gradus V).

Rectius'tam...quam' exprimit aequalitatem SIMPLICEM (as...as); 'tam...ut' introducit clausulam consecutivam (so...that) cum coniunctivo.

Formae similes ('tam') sed functiones distinctae: comparatio directa vs. clausula subordinata consecutiva.

Summarium

Recognitio

  • Quantō...tantō (vel quō...eō): 'the more...the more', ambo ablativi mensurae.
  • Tam...quam: aequalitas ('as...as'), distinctum ab tam...ut (clausula consecutiva).
  • Quam + superlativus: 'as...as possible'.
  • Ut...ita: correlativum concessivum ('while...yet').
